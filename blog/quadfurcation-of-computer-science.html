We try to teach towards the big ideas and intellectual concepts we anticipate for the future rather than those of the past in our course. Our topic this week is Python programming in particular and Computer Science in general. Computer Science seems to be undergoing a dramatic realignment of sorts that we touched upon today.

Wikipedia.org decomposes Computer Science according the traditional divisions of Theoretical, Systems and Applications. What I proposed today in class is three de-facto changes I sense not adequately represented in this taxonomy.

First, Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning has transcended it's traditional subdivision under Applications in terms of both size and import. It is the hottest topic attracting the most interests from CS students today and requires a very different skill set from what the typical CS major studied even a decade or two ago.
