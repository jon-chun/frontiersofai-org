Here's an interesting Microsoft Research Podcast by Dr. Andrew Phillips about Programming Biology. Microsoft has had groups dedicated to Biological Computation for over a decade, but the interface between computational studies/biology is a bit janky. Despite the hope and hype about a more formalized theoretical reframing of biology in terms of computational models, progress on finding a generalized paradigm to bring computational paradigms to biology has been slow/halting. However, within this sector some specialized niches like drug discovery, sequencing/searching and laboratory automation have proven to be effective albeit along more statistical and experimental terms.

In the podcast, Dr. Phillips mentioned several resources for programming biology:

  • Michael Pedersen (an intern who helped develop ) Old home page , linkedin page as well as research paper and slides on project
  • RAIN, Reasoning About Interaction Networks
  • Visual GEC (Genetic Engineering of Cells) Software
  • Visual DSD (DNA Strand Displacement)
