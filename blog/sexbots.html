The podcast "This Week in ML & AI" with Sam Charington had an interesting podcast on "Human-Robot Interaction and Empthy" with Kate Darling (8 Aug 2019). It's an interesting conversation that discusses the topic from a variety of perspectives including:

  • Our evolutionary propensity to projecting human-like qualities onto ML/AI systems/robots
  • The artificial division between how we project human qualities rights onto various animals (eating horses vs cows) and how these map onto how we perceive different robotic systems
  • Educating children to interact with robots and how our own morality is affected by how we interact with robots
  • Sexbots and the ethics of in-game purchases, loot crates
