I just finished the trade book Bayesian Analysis with Python, 2nd ed. by Osvaldo Martin. It's a quick but relatively comprehensive overview of Bayesian modeling and many related subjects illustrated with Python and the libraries PyMC3 and ArviZ. It's good if you're familiar with the statistics and math but less so with Bayesian and

The ending chapter 'Where to Go Next' gives a good roadmap of other good resources to learn Bayesian statistics and modeling. Here is my reordered and augmented version of his self-learning roadmap on Bayesian topics:

  • Think Bayes by Allen B. Downey (GitHub repo in PyMC3/Python for upcoming 2nd edition)
  • Probabilistic Programming and Bayesian Analysis for Hackers by (Github repo in PyMC3/Python)
  • Statistical Rethinking by Richard McElreath (with GitHub repo in Stan/R and PyMC3/Python)
