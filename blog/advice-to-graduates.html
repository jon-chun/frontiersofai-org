Advice to Graduates

Home » Blog » Advice to Graduates
Posted by jonchunMay 28, 2019

With graduation season is winding down, I came across an item that deserves to be memorialized in blog post form.

The Knowledge Project podcast is occasionally exceptional and this podcast featuring Thomas Tull titled ‘Following Intellectual Curiosity” is one such episode. It’s not just Tull’s personal story and remarkably consistent success across so many fields. He maintains a concise, practical and thoughtful level of interaction with his interviewer throughout a long interview ranging across topics from startups to parenting to the film production process.

Of course, I have to agree with Tull’s book recommendation on Marcus Aurelius’ Meditations as well. He fits the mold of a number of successful business people who cite this work as inspirational (and consoling), and I’ve handed out a number of copies myself this time of year.

Finally, a video recommendation of the now-famous commencement speech by Steve Jobs on what really matters in life…
