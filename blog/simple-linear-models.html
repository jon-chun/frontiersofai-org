Simple Linear Models Draft

Home » Blog » Simple Linear Models
Created: 2019-04-22 • Status: draft

Although DNN and Bayesian Networks are the new black, it seems people are overlooking the power in simple linear models - especially when they are used and extended by more sophisticated use. I came across this excellent talk (w/slides) by Vincent Warmerdam on this very topic.

I emphasize to my students that Occam's Razor and Laziness as a Programmer's Virtue are as true as ever. Careful reading beyond the marketing sizzle tends to indicate that RegEx is used in many NLP production systems and XGBoost/scikit-learn linear models in many data science operations.
