With the next Presidential election cycle approximately a year away now is a good time to reflect on the State of the Art (SOTA) in DeepFake technologies. Fake News is also There will be a growing number of articles on this subject This will help us extrapolate as to what potential 'October Surprises' we can anticipate for the November 2020 election.

The original meaning of DeepFakes denotes generated images/videos of objects (usually people) that do not exist. One of the most popular github repos offers software (faceswap) for do-it-yourself DeepFakes.

My use of 'DeepFakes' here is more general in that it includes any generated data like image, video, voice and text. My use of 'DeepFakes' here also is more specific in that it describes the use of fake media that is based upon a real person or entity with the intent of strategically misrepresenting that person/entity for particular ends. For example, a famous politician could be made to appear saying or doing outrageous things to undermine their supporters, mobilize opposition, or otherwise

It looks like the battle against DeepFakes is evolving along the lines of the state of permanent war between anti/virus forces. DARPA is sponsoring research into software to detect DeepFakes where there is already academic interest and some fairly sophisticated solutions (Grover). At the same, time technological progress is making DeepFakes more difficult to detect. It will become a constant technological arms race. Unfortunately, the mere presence of DeepFakes can powerfully exploit existing Cognitive Biases and reinforce Mass Media Framing regardless of the ground truth.
