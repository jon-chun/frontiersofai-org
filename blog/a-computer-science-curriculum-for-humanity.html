A Computer Science Curriculum for Humanity Draft

Home » Blog » A Computer Science Curriculum for Humanity
Created: 2019-03-12 • Status: draft

For the past five years I've been focused on developing a new Computer Science Curriculum that breaks from the traditional narrow STEM-centric model by exploiting rapid changes in technology and needs of society. Over the past three years, with the support of numerous individuals at Kenyon College, we've been able to prove these ideas out in the classroom and gather valuable feedback for future improvements. This post introduces some of the highlights from our experiences.

By nature I am more of an Entrepreneur than an Academic and the unlikely situation of someone like me working on this project like this is an accident of many coincidences. When we started our family about a dozen years ago my wife accepted a faculty position at Kenyon College and I moved from being a startup entrepreneur and Director for the world's largest security company in Silicon Valley to a father in a village of 600 surrounding a small liberal arts college in rural Ohio.
