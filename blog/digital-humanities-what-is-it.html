Digital Humanities: What is It? Draft

Home » Blog » Digital Humanities: What is It?
Created: 2019-05-02 • Status: draft

Digital Humanities is a very different beast depending upon whom you ask.

  • Some Humanists view it as a neoliberal power grab that undermines the traditional liberal arts and convert it into tech training grounds for private industry
  • Some Humanists argue that technology is fundamentally incompatible with the Humanities and what little DH research has been done is self-evident, trivial or flat out wrong. in a boondoggle whose time has passed..
  • Many in STEM, including Computer Science, think technology liberates DH from domain experts and that anyone can feed various type of data like text or image through algorithms and visualizations to search for unusual patterns
  • Other stakeholders see DH as a way to revitalize and make the liberal arts more relevant in an age when technological literacy and technology itself is increasingly at the heart of every issue in society

All of the above have some kernels of truth but albeit wrapped within larger husks of personal and professional biases. Unfortunately, the extremes from both ends of the spectrum trends towards ill-informed and/or and that not only inflames the legitimate criticisms of other stakeholders but also their deep set counter-biases.

Although the arrow of progress points to a wider and deeper dissemination of technology into the Liberal Arts as into every other area of society, we may have reached peak 'Digital Humanities' several years ago. While we see more thoughtful, complex and mature DH scholarship and growing research/conference metrics, there remains a wariness about the term 'Digital Humanities'.
