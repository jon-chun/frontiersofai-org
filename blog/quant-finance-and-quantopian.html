There are great resources for learning the core concepts in quantitative finance, financial engineering and quantitative econometrics. The required background tends towards applied mathematics commonly found in either Engineering, Physics or Applied Maths/Statistics. Here are a few of my favorites:

  • Quantopian (Python)
  • QuantEcon (Python and Julia) by Thomas Sargent
  • WorldQuant University (free)
  • A Github Awesome List of QuantFin Libraries/Frameworks and Awesome QuantFin Learning Resources
