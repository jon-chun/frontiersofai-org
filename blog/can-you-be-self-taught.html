Created: 2019-07-24 • Status: draft

Society seems at a cross roads today. On the one hand we live in an increasingly 'credentialized' world where both subspeciality degrees and legal requirements proliferate. On the other hand there are fewer barriers to learning and more opportunities to demonstrate proficiency outside of traditional educational tracks.

Examples, doctor/lawyer to manicurist

Breaksdown between organizational complexity and naked execution.

This is especially true in the technical world for a variety of reasons.

  • Programming: Github, Hackathons, Technical Interviews
  • Data Science: Kaggle
  • AI/ML: fast.ai, Google
