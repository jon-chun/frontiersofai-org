Computer Science for the Rest of Us Draft

Home » Blog » Computer Science for the Rest of Us
Created: 2019-07-02 • Status: draft

Computer Science is a field in transition and is bifurcating into a field of (1) hard-core theory for tool creators and (2) a general literacy of computational thinking increasingly ubiquitous among the 'rest of us' tool users.

The middle-ground between these two extremes is enjoying a heyday right now although there are worrying trends.
