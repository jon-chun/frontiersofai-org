Time Series Draft

Home » Blog » Time Series
Created: 2019-08-12 • Status: draft

This is a subject that doesn't get enough coverage in many AI forums given the disproportionate impact they have in more traditional ML/statistical applications like econometrics and engineering modeling. I have noticed an uptick over the past few years in interest on this topic in more traditional AI/software conferences, but that may be a personal cognitive bias based upon my own research.

Here are a few great resources I've been found and have been following recently.

  • Practical Time Series Analysis, Aileen Nielson, O'Reilly 2019
  • Forecasting: Principles and Practice, Rob J Hyndman and George Athanasopoulos
  • How to Create an ARIMA Model for Time Series Forecasting in Python, Jason Brownlee, Jan 2017 (Book)
  • Time Series Analysis, AileenNielsen, PyCon 2017 (github repo) May 2017 (Simple Review of Python, Pandas, etc for TS)
  • Ch3 Anomaly Detection Machine Learning and Security, Clarence Chio and Freedman
  • Statistical Forecasting: Notes on Regression and Time Series, Robert Nau, Duke Fuqua School of Business
  • Galit Schmueli, Papers and Videos

Forecasting With the Theta Method by Kostas I. Nikolopoulos and Dimitrios D. Thomakos (Wiley 2019): ARIMA, ETS, Theta
