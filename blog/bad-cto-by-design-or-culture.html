Bad CTO by Design or Culture Draft

Home » Blog » Bad CTO by Design or Culture
Created: 2019-08-08 • Status: draft

Quantitative minds reflexively tend toward seeking elegant, concise, and generalized abstractions that can model, explain, predict and even effect the world and future states. Unfortunately, models are necessarily simplistic reductions of reality and can go wrong in so many ways as any Data Scientists knows. CTOs work at a higher level of abstraction and work with a more limited number of technical models largely accumulated

in their heads that also can become dangerously disconnected from 'reality' when it comes to major IT initiatives.
