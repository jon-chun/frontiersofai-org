Welcome to the blog for the third iteration of our course titled ‘Programming Humanity’ in the Spring of 2019 at Kenyon College.

In this course we take a serious approach to studying both the theoretical as well as practical foundations of Computational Thinking as applied across the Humanities and beyond.  In particular, we tend to focus on projects related to the many major social issues intimately entwined with technology from AI to Bioinformatics.

Our mission is to define a new standard of excellence for Computational Humanities at the small Liberal Arts college with a deep, interdisciplinary and mutually respectful approach to scholarship from both worlds.

Please contact us if you have any questions or comments.
