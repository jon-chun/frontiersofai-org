Created: 2019-06-29 • Status: draft

Let's explore three different schools of thought and attempt to reconcile them:

  1. "You do not really understand something unless you can explain it
  2. That which I cannot do I do not understand
  3. Those who can do, those who can't teach

One of the best things about teaching is that it is one of the more efficient modes of learning. This is particularly true for more objectively quantitative fields where truth is less ambigious and can be arrived at independently.

In my course I have a limited amount of complexity I permit myself to introduce.
