Teaching AI - Opening Day Draft

Home » Blog » Teaching AI - Opening Day
Created: 2019-08-30 • Status: draft

Tomorrow is the first class in the third iteration of our 'AI for the Humanities class at Kenyon College. We believe this is the first AI course specifically created for a broad range of the Liberal Arts. Typical courses in this area either elide over the technology to focus aspects like ethics or culture or focus on CS with the a patina of Humanities. We are able to provide a new course that equally emphasizes both the technology and Humanities aspects of AI In part, this is because until recently the technical barriers were just too high to productively teach and interesting class in AI to a general/mixed class.
