Topic: Algorithms (Fall 2020)

Home » Fall 2020 Archive » Topic: Algorithms (Fall 2020)
Created: 2021-08-29 • Modified: 2021-08-29 • Archived from programminghumanity.wordpress.com

This week we learn about the origins of computer science dating back to Aristotle and influenced by the fields of both logic and linguistics. We'll also explore the connections that are being made between human cognition and and computers. Is human cognitive processing algorithmic?

In terms of code, we'll learn how Computer Science represents and implements one familiar aspect of cognition: algorithmic thinking. We'll study this with examples of specific algorithms (e.g. search) and a general pattern of computation (e.g. recursion).  We'll also explore measures of algorithmic space-time complexity through BigO notation.

We close the week by focusing on the one aspect of human cognition that is still a mystery: consciousness. Can consciousness be explained mathematically, materially, or spiritually? Will science ever be able to explain consciousness, or is the qualitative nature of experience unquantifiable?
