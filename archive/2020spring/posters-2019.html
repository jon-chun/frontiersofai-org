IPHS 300 AI for the Humanities

Integrated Program for Humane Studies

Summary of Spring 2019 Final Posters (Descriptions and Links Below).

NB: With the addition of QR status to this course in 2019, all course projects have been converted to quantitative posters format. Previous essay format are no longer an option for IPSH200 class projects.

Centaur Screenwriting with 345M GPT-2: The Problems and Possibilities of AI-Generated Creative Content for the Screen [Exploring and analyzing the creative screenwriting possibilities with generative text AI] Liv Kane

Evaluating AI Music Generation with Performance RNN in Magenta TensorFlow [An comparative analysis of AI-generated music grounded in music theory] Spalding Vance

The Influence of Artificial Intelligence in Dance Choreography (paper) [Examining the shifting boundaries, theory and ethics of various dance projects integrating AI] Abby Plone

Synthetic Biology: Analyzing Trends in Intellectual Property Rights vs Open Access to Research [Correlation analysis between research publications and patent time series to forecast trends in open vs close access in Synthetic Biology research] Ebie Quinn

Donald Trump in Modern Comedy: Or How Trump Jokes are not Funny [Synchronizing skit comedy punchlines with audio spectrographs to identify diminishing humor trends in SNL Trump skits] Max D. Humphrey

Adapting to an Artificially Intelligent World (paper) [Public policy considerations for AI: Surveillance and Control as well as Labor Markets] Jack Budington

Homomorphic Encryption [Detailed survey of encryption scheme that preserves privacy while enabling the benefits of big data analytics on sensitive data like healthcare information] Emily Rachfal

Is the Music I Listen to as "Kosher" as I Think? The Age-Old Debate Between Current and Older Generations [An intergenerational study in music tastes based upon NLP analysis of lyrics of the 1980s vs 2010s] Patrick O'Leary

International Genetically Engineered Machine (iGem) Competition for the Liberal Arts [Introduction to the largest and fastest growing international Synthetic Biology competition with proposal for inaugural Kenyon team] Shane Canfield

Does U.S. Conflict Intervention Provoke Terrorism in the Middle East and North Africa [Analyzing time series correlation between US interventions and terrorist activity in the Islamic world] Savannah Overly

Design-Driven Collaboration @ Kenyon: A Model for Innovative Learning [Adopting the successful model of design-driven innovation centers to the environment of Small Liberal Arts] Alexander Gow

Fake Tweets: Using Twitter API and Natural Language Processing to Analyze Twitter Users [Using NLP to analyze differences between real and parody Trump tweets] Ceylan Can

How Far is Too Far? A Sentiment Analysis of Controversy [Detecting controversy around Game of Thrones topics by analyzing contemporaneous tweets ] Patrick Craig

LACIFA: Intelligently matching underrepresented students with Small Liberal Arts Colleges [Discovering new opportunities in higher education by personalizing search and discovery] Liam Ingoglia

Suicide Rates in the United States from 1985-2015 [Using data analytics to study rising trends in US suicide rates] Dylan Goodwin

Automating the Socratic Method: How AI Might Change How We Approach Philosophy [Using sentence-level semantic embeddings to approximate Socratic dialogs] Nick Downey

The New News Breakdown: What You Missed When You Were Out of the Simulator [A 2040's speech by human rights lawyer and genetic engineering advocate before the House vote on Eugenic Rights] Erin Shaheen

Women Representation: STEM Education [Data Analytics of wage gap using R and Shiny Web Server] Ghada Bakbouk
