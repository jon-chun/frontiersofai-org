Created: 2020-08-29 • Modified: 2021-08-26 • Archived from programminghumanity.wordpress.com

Data, especially Big Data, is the foundation and fuel upon which many of the technologies like AI and Bioinformatics are revolutionizing society.  This is why data and and an intimate understanding of it forms the introductory foundation of our course that covers algorithms to analytics to AI.

Data is not simply a collection of inert objective measurements of reality.  Rather, you will come to appreciate the inherent limitations of data selection, acquisition, cleaning and analysis.  You will also understand how conscious and unconscious biases work to adulterate how we interpret data.  Finally, you will explore the validity of any truth represented in data itself.
