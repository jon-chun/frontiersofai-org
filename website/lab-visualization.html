Lab: Visualization

Fall 2026 • Updated 2019-01-28

This week will be studying best (and worst) practices of data visualizations and interactive visual storytelling through data. We'll be exploring and critiquing a wide range of visualization and storytelling from data journalism and the Digital Humanities.

Laptop Configuration:

Please register for a free account on Tableau Public as well as download and configure the free client software for your laptop. Lab laptops will be available for anyone who needs one.

Class Projects:

Our explorations of data and visualizations this week should provide many fertile ideas for class projects. All students should begin to start thinking about potential Digital Humanities ideas now.

Consult with Professors Chun and Elkins about how to design a meaningful Digital Humanities research project. We will also help you identify the most appropriate technologies and tools along with a realistic assessment of timeframes so you can successfully complete your project on time. Remember, all projects must be approved by course instructors by the dates listed in the syllabus.

For those with more programming experience who are interesting in more original and programmatically expression visualizations, we have many technical recommendations available from JavaScript to R/Python libraries to alternative web frameworks like React.
