Summary of Spring 2020 Final Posters (Descriptions and Links Below).

Writing the Next American Hit: Using GPT-2 to Explore the Possibility of Creating Successful AI-Generated Song Lyrics [Evaluating song lyrics generated by the GPT-2 language model fine-tuned on a corpus of Taylor Swift lyrics] Sophie Barrio

GPT-2 AI Poetry Generation: Writing like Donne [Analyzing the metrical form of classical poetry generated by GPT-2 trained on John Donne] Kaiya Case

COVID-19: Global Trends in Social Protection, Unemployment, and Economic Stimuli [Visualizing the effectiveness of COVID-19 economic stimulus packages via changes in unemployment rates ] Mara Kaspers

#Protests and @realDonaldTrump: Public Perception of COVID-19 Lockdown Protests on Twitter [Explore the potential causal effects of Donald Trumps Tweets on mobilizing COVID-19 protests] Molly Kavanaugh

The Body and The Machine: Functionalities and Failures of AI-Generated Comedy [Exploring GPT-2’s ability to generate Oscar Wilde’s distinctive ‘comedies of manners’ style of comedy] Rebecca Turner

345M-GPT-2 After James Wright: Can AI Generate Convincing Contemporary Poetry? [Characterizing the ability of GPT-2 to generate poetry in the style of James Wright including metaphors, imagery, and formal structures] Jonah Zitelli

Taxonomy Techniques for Holocaust-Related Image Digitization and Text [Outline a technical pipeline and systematic classification taxonomy to unify disparate Holocaust-Related sources into a coherent digitized research corpus] Sejin Kim

Sentiment Analysis Amidst the COVID-19 Pandemic: Comparing the Emotional Valence of President Trump and Governor Cuomo’s Coronavirus Briefings [Use sentiment analysis to compare and interpret the public speeches of President Trump and Governor Cuomo during the first several months of the COVID-19 pandemic] Davida Harris

Building a Universal Human Trafficking Lexicon [Create a universal lexicon to assist in the fight against human trafficking by unifying disparate structures of extant trafficking datasets] Nora Mittleman

Social Media and Popularism in the Primaries: The Sanders Campaign in the Context of Two Decades [Exploring the effects of popularism in swing states through social media and two Bernie Sander’s Persidential Campaigns] Guilia Cancro
