Robots, Bots and Automation – Engineering
M Class:           Robotics, Automation and Lethal Autonomous Weapons (LAWs)
W Lab:             Ro/Botics
F Discussion:    Automation & LAWs:  A Peaceful Life of Leisure or a Dystopian Nightmare

This week we begin the study of increasingly more complex and interconnected applications of technology. We’ll look at both bots and robotics as well as how they are beginning to impact everything from potential long-term structural unemployment to automated killing weapons.

Many concepts from previous weeks from data and programming to cognitive biases and machine learning will come into play when discussing the many implementations and ramifications of automation and ro/bot technologies.  In parallel, many questions about humanity are brought into sharp relief by the rapid progress and introduction of these technologies as well such as fundamental geopolitical shifts, potential massive economic dislocations and inequalities, and basic legal and ethical questions.

Discussion Questions:

  • Humans can sometimes project human qualities on non-human objects they interact with in their environment like pets and computers.  To meet this demand, AI and robotics researchers often try to anthropomorphize technology via Voice Interfaces (VUI) or biped robots with human faces and gestures.  Often this makes interactions more intuitive and effective.  Are there scenarios where we should intentionally design AI or robots to be clearly non-human?  In which scenarios and why?
  • Last week we read about the influential Libet study that suggest that human free will is more illusionary than real. Perhaps there is a spectrum across humanity regarding the degree of free will.  If robots are developed that can be objectively proven to have more free will than some humans (e.g. addicts or OCD disorders) should they earn rights equivalent to their degree of free will?  Why or why not?
  • As AI and robotics evolve will there be a limit to the traditional “human rights” they have access to?  Should AI/robotics, even if objectively proven to be just as sentient as humans, forever be enslaved and subjected to dirty, dull and dangerous work in service of humans?  Is there never a degree of evolution AI/robotics can achieve that justify them earning some form of human rights?
  • What do you feel about AI/robots developing emotional intelligence?  Humans constantly lie, persuade or otherwise try to influence others for their own benefit, so what is wrong with AI/robots doing the same?  Can you think of any rules or regulations (e.g. transparency or liability laws) that could make the use of affective computing tolerable or even preferable to the human variety?
  • Humans have developed a number of laws to protect the most vulnerable of our society such as child neglect, elder abuse or predatory lending practices especially designed to protect the young, poor and the underrepresented minorities.  How do we calibrate just how aggressive, intelligent and effective our AI/robotic technologies can be with various segments of society to mirror these laws?
  • Former US Justice Dept Lawyer and current Professor John Yoo at Berkeley Law School recently made the argument that we may want not want to rule out using assassin drone technology in a first strike scenario.  After all, surgically killing one man or a group of leaders of an enemy state is objectively more humane than the massive loss of life, property and material that comes with conventional warfare.  What do you think of this reasoning?  Can you think of qualifications that could improve this position?
  • Technology creates a psychological isolation and distance from the consequences of war at the same time it gives technologically advanced nations immense leverage over other nations.  Given the growing popular appeal of resorting to automated warfare for politicians and militaries (e.g. minimal casualties, costs, quicker wins, less collateral damage, better PR) can you think of a way to retard the logical slide of increasingly automated tech warfare detached from any public awareness or understanding?
