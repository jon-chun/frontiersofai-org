Networks – Political Science

M Class:              OS and Networks

W Lab:                UNIX, Internet Protocols, APIs, PKI, Bitcoin/Blockchain, StuxNet Malware

F Discussion:      Hacking and Augustine Just Cyberwar

In the first three weeks we studied prototypical examples of the three main components of a computing system:  Input(Data) -> Processing(Algorithms) -> Output(Visualizations).  This week, we’ll combine and encapsulate these three concepts into a single abstraction we’ll call a “Process”.

Furthermore, we’ll demonstrate how this abstract Process concept is physically instantiated within a physical computer under the control and coordination of an Operating System (OS).  We’ll see how both Processes and OSes are evolving across both physical and virtual devices via virtualization and containerization within and parallelization across computing devices. Finally, we’ll contextualize all the previous concepts within the idea of networking – the fabric of the Internet and our most sophisticated computational and AI systems.

We’ll use our knowledge of Operating Systems and Networking to explore technological threats in the areas of the Darknet, Viruses/Malware and Cyber Warfare.  In particular we’ll do a forensic examination of the StuxNet worm and explore Wikileaks to acquire a more informed and critical opinion of the nature and extent of such threats as well as how to maintain an open society in the face of such real and potential threats.
