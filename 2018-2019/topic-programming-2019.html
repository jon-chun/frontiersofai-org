Programming Paradigms – Music & Maths
M Class:              Evolution and Paradigms in Programming
W Lab:                Encoding, Visualizing and Processing Sound
F Discussion:      Aesthetics and Computational Creativity

We use this week to catch our breath and ground ourselves by focusing on practical programming skills in Python.  This will not only give context to much of the theory we have learned so far, but will also prepare us for incorporating more code in our upcoming topics, mini-projects, and final course project.

To accommodate the diversity of programming experiences in our class we have two parallel sets of readings on Monday.  Students only need to do one of these two options.  For those new to programming or who haven’t taken our ‘AI for the Humanities’ course we have curated a few of the best intro to Python courses for you.  For more experienced programmers, we have a different but hopefully equally interesting Python learning assignment focused on getting you beyond the conceptional ‘beginner’/’hacker’ hump.

In class, we’ll use your newfound Python skills to explore a variety of seemingly creative or artistic projects of various sophistication.
