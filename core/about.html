More about the course

This course was designed by Jon Chun along with Katherine Elkins in 2016. Since then hundreds of students have taken the course and gone on to work in a variety of fields. Many of their projects can be found on digital.kenyon.edu/dh. Some have been downloaded over 1000 times from institutions around the world.
