Logo Design

Home » Core » Logo Design
Created: 2019-02-07

We are visual animals living in an age of information and sensory overload. This makes creating a memorable logo all the more important for effective and memorable communication. Plus, design work like this gives me a creative break from reading about Bayesian Graph Models and Financial Time Series Models in Julia all day.

Here are two logos I’ve been considering for related projects I’m working on. My first logo is to brand a Digital Humanities web page about the DH courses, research and student projects I’m working on at Kenyon College. It’s derived from a second logo I created for a project of broader scope I’m also developing.

The original design in this logo series is what I affectionately title the ‘Naked Ape KL’ logo. This logo is a bit less intuitive and will probably undergo a few more iterations, but the symbolism is there.
