Reinforcement Learning and Communicating Complexity

Home » Core » Reinforcement Learning and Communicating Complexity
Created: 2019-01-27 • Modified: 2019-01-28

Deep Reinforcement Learning was arguably the single hottest area of AI that I taught last semester in our ‘AI for Humanists’ course. I am always on the look out for comprehensive, concise and illuminating material on our course topics. For difficult topics like Deep RL I am usually fortunate if I can find material that satisfy 2 of these 3 goals.

I just came across a terrific 2016 presentation by Richard Sutton at a Microsoft Research conference. Prof Sutton with co-author Prof Andrew Barto wrote what some consider the ‘Bible’ of Reinforcement Learning. Prof Sutton admirable covers all three requirements above and does so in an interactive, engaging and entertaining fashion as you can see below.

Several days ago Lex Fridman also released a lecture on Deep RL from his class on AI at MIT. Prof Fridman gives an amazing example of how one can overcome the ‘pick 2 of the 3’ dilemma described above. This has got to rank among one of the best lectures I’ve seen on a complex topic like Deep RL.
