Programming, Language and Thought

This week we study artificial (programming) languages and compare them to natural languages (e.g. English). Borrowing from cognitive science and the philosophy of language, we’ll see important ways language and thought influence and shape each other. In earlier weeks we’ve considered the ways our access to and interpretation of information/data can affect how we describe a problem and how we solve it. This week we’ll see how distinct programming paradigms can also facilitate or constrain our ability to find solutions to a problem.

Finally, we’ll briefly introduce, review and tie together several Python libraries that will come in handy for both upcoming Mini-Projects and your final class project. We’ll add the Pandas library to our Python toolbox that will help us clean up messy datasets for our next Mini-Project: Data Wrangling.
